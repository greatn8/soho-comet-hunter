Prepare a native CCOR report

This opens a report reviewer using the original 2048 × 1920 FITS pixels. Mark the same object in at least three frames, then copy the coordinates into Sungrazer with Upper Left as the origin.

The NOAA quicklook is rotated for viewing. Native reporting measures the original frames directly, so that display rotation does not affect your report coordinates.

1. Prepare on Bourbaki

Paste this command into your Bourbaki terminal. It reads the candidate’s measured times and retrieves up to six matching original frames. Downloads and any extra Python environment use temporary storage. Your completed HTML reviewer stays in your home folder.

2. Open the reviewer on your computer

3. Measure and export

Use the frame arrows and zoom to inspect the object. Click its centre in at least three frames. Check both the original view and temporal contrast, then choose Copy report or Download report.

The export includes image size, coordinate origin, native FITS pixel references, exact observation times and original filenames. It prepares your measurements; you decide whether the moving source warrants a comet report. If the originals are no longer on NOAA’s rolling feed, preparation stops and requires archive FITS files.
